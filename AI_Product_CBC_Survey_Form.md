# AI-Assisted Digital Textbooks for Learners with Hearing Loss

*Preliminary market survey*

This survey explores preferences for different features of AI-assisted digital learning resources for learners with hearing loss. You will be shown a series of textbook options, each with different combinations of learning features. There are no right or wrong answers. Please select the option you would most prefer for deaf learners in each choice set.

Estimated time: 10 to 15 minutes.
